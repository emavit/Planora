# Planora identity

Clarity, from plan to progress.

Planora helps people see the work, connect the dates and keep moving. The identity combines a compact schedule-bar mark with a restrained wordmark. Three staggered bars suggest progress over time; a separate dot represents a milestone.

## Visual system

- Deep indigo: #272C68. Brand foundation.
- Iris: #5264D9. Icon gradient endpoint.
- Aqua: #80E0D2. Accent, not body text on white.
- Graphite: #1D1D1F. Light-theme typography.
- Mist: #F5F5F7. Light workspace backdrop.
- Typography: platform system fonts, semibold headings, sentence case.

Use the mark at 24px or larger in product chrome; the Windows icon includes 16–256px variants. Leave at least one bar-height of clear space around promotional logo placements. Do not recolor task bars to match the brand: project formatting and status remain meaningful.

## Product language and compatibility

Keep Planora as the display name, executable identity and .planora extension. Retain existing settings/recovery paths and .mpp workflows. This is a visual rebrand, not a format migration. Keep the approved Apple-inspired surfaces and Project-style task/Gantt arrangement. Use short action labels and specific feedback; avoid marketing copy in editing flows.

## Assets

The app header and window icon share AppBrand.RenderIcon. Generate PNG/ICO using `Planora --brand-assets <folder>`. The source implementation owns this original geometry; no third-party artwork is included. The brand is a working product identity, not a claim of trademark clearance.
